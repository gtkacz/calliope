Describe the throne room where Queen Maelith holds court — make it feel cold and absolute.
Calliope

The Throne of Null occupies the precise centre of Maelith's great hall — a hall so vast that its vaulted ceiling dissolves into a darkness no torch has ever reached. The seat itself is carved from a single vein of obsidian-black basalt, polished to a mirror sheen that reflects nothing warmly: every face that approaches it sees its own distortion stretched long and pale, like a warning lore/maelith.md .

Cold air pools here even in summer. The floor is laid in alternating slabs of chalk-white marble and the same black stone as the throne, so that walking toward it feels like crossing a board in some eternal game no petitioner chose to enter. No warmth escapes the room — not firelight, not breath, not mercy. Maelith receives her court seated perfectly still, hands folded at the wrists, and the silence she enforces is not the silence of restraint but of absolute authority made ambient, as permanent and impersonal as stone.
