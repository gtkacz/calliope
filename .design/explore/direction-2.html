Describe the throne room where Queen Maelith holds court — make it feel cold and absolute.
Calliope

The throne room of the Obsidian Court is built to unmake the self. Its walls rise forty feet of polished basalt, so perfectly mirrored that attendants see their own pale faces retreating into infinity on either side — a reminder that the queen is singular, and all else merely reflection. No torches burn here; light seeps instead from thin veins of glacial luminite threaded through the stone, casting the chamber in a blue-white radiance as cold and sourceless as starlight. lore/maelith.md

At its far end, the throne itself: a single block of uncarved obsidian, seat and back cut by no chisel — pulled whole from the volcanic heart of the Ashcaldera and dragged here across three hundred miles of salted plain. Queen Maelith never descends to meet her petitioners. She sits motionless in that jagged dark, and the room does the speaking for her. To stand before the Obsidian Court is to understand, without being told, that the cold is her mercy, and silence her most generous reply.
