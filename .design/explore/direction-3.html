The Obsidian Court
A21 · Saga of Ash

Describe the throne room where Queen Maelith holds court — make it feel cold and absolute.
Calliope

The Throne of Ash is not a seat — it is an accusation hewn from the mountain itself, carved from obsidian quarried at Vael's Crossing where three rivers once ran warm and now run black. It rises seventeen steps above the hall's floor, each riser etched with the names of those who swore fealty and later learned what that word costs. lore/maelith.md No fires burn within the chamber. The cold is not a failure of maintenance; it is policy.

Light enters only through a single clerestory slit, forty feet above, oriented so that at the hour of the morning audience, one pale shaft falls precisely across Maelith's left eye — leaving the right in shadow. Courtiers have learned never to look directly at it. The floor is polished to a mirror finish and reflects the vault above, so petitioners walk across their own upended sky. The effect is not accidental. Every choice in this room is architecture serving a single argument: that the queen is the only fixed point in a world built to make you feel you are falling.
